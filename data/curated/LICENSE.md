# Curated entries: CC0-1.0

The entries in this directory are dedicated to the public domain under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication:
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

They were checked on real Windows installs by the Resource Manager project.
Steam app IDs and names come from Steam's official most-played charts, and
WinGet package IDs come from microsoft/winget-pkgs (MIT). Both are used as
facts.
